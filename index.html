How It Works

Getting started. Tap Start Camera. The video fills the screen (the whole picture always shows; black bars only where the shapes don't match). The camera records nonstop in the background, and nothing is uploaded.

Your live controls. Every control is a see-through block on a grid over the video. Tap Layout to arrange them: drag to move, ↻ changes shape or turns a slider, ⤡ stretches sliders, × removes, + Add opens the catalog of every control (new controls show up there). If the edit bar is in the way, drag it by its ✥ grip (double-tap the grip to send it back to the top). Tap Done to save. There's a separate layout for phone and iPad, upright and sideways, and each block stays pinned to its nearest corner. Export shares your layouts as a file (also copied); a friend uses Import file or Paste. The see-through slider is in the edit bar and in Settings.

Record every play. Start Play when the teams line up, End Play at the whistle. Each play is saved as its own numbered clip in the current game. Last opens the most recent play; tapping it during a play ends and shows that play. Replay looks back at the last few seconds even if you didn't start a play.

Down & distance. Tap the down on the Downs block (1st resets to 10 yards), or Goal. Set yards with the Yards slider or Yards + / −. The next play you start gets that label. When a play ends the down moves on by one (4th goes to 1st & 10), and a gold Undo shows for 3 seconds for timeouts.

Special teams. Add the Kickoff · Punt · FG · PAT block from + Add (it's also in the down & distance panel). Tap one before the play to label it; tap it again to turn it off. Set the down first for a punt or field goal (4th & 8 · Punt). A touchdown sets up the PAT, a PAT or made field goal sets up the kickoff, and a kickoff or punt sets up 1st & 10. Field goals and PATs get Good / No good buttons in the result panel. There are also one-tap Kickoff, Punt, Field goal and PAT blocks, and if you forgot to mark a punt, tap Punt in the result panel after the play.

What happened on the play. When you tap End Play, a big panel pops up. Use the big − / + (or −5, +5, +10, +20) for yards gained, or tap Incomplete, 1st Down, Touchdown, Penalty, Turnover or No play / timeout. It shows the next down & distance and sets it for you (1st & 10 plus 4 yards becomes 2nd & 6; gaining the distance resets to 1st & 10; a penalty or no play keeps the same down). Tap Done, or Start next play right from the panel. Reopen it from the Play result block, or from Result at the top while watching a play. You can turn the pop-up off in Settings.

While a play records, controls you don't need (result, replay, freeze, tray, camera flip, settings, help, game, quarter, layout) are dimmed and switched off so they can't be bumped. Downs, yards, tags, zoom, photo and Last stay live.

Player tags. Enter jersey numbers in Settings. The Tag players block tags the play being recorded (or the last play). Tag your kid's number and the play is starred as a keeper automatically. While watching a play, the number chips at the top tag it too. In the tray, tap a number to see just those plays.

Game. The Game block opens opponent, quarter and score, plus New game. The Quarter block moves to the next quarter with one tap. Plays are numbered per game, grouped by game in the tray, and saved clips show the quarter and score.

Watching a replay. Left side: Live, tray, loop, speed, back to start, frame back/forward, play/pause, zoom. Right side: drawing tools, color, undo, clear, Photo, Save Clip, 9:16. Tap the video to pause/play, drag across it to scrub, pinch to zoom. Steady smooths shaky footage. Drawings stick to the moment you drew them and the replay pauses there briefly.

Saving. Photo saves the frame with drawings. Save Clip saves the replay as video with drawings, down & distance, quarter and score written on. 9:16 makes a vertical clip or photo for social media, cropped to what's on screen. In the tray: ★ marks keepers, Save opens the share sheet (choose Save Video / Save Image), Save keepers to Photos does them all, Highlight reel stitches the plays you're looking at into one video with a title card before each, and Import from Photos brings in videos and pictures to mark up.

Keep your plays safe. Plays stay in this browser's storage on this device, not in Photos, until you save them. Safari can erase a website's stored data if it isn't opened for about 7 days, and clearing website data erases it too. Open the app from its Home Screen icon, and after every game star your keepers and tap Save keepers to Photos.

Add to Home Screen. Safari: Share button (square with up arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add. Chrome on iPhone/iPad: Share in the address bar → Add to Home Screen. Then always open it from the Sentinels Live icon. It runs full-screen and keeps its storage more reliably.

Steadier video. Use 1080p, zoom less while filming (zoom in on the replay instead), brace the device, or use a tripod clamp. Web pages can't use Apple's camera stabilization; Steady helps on replays.

Keyboard. Enter start/end play · Space replay / play-pause · ←→ frame · 123 speed · Esc live · F freeze · C clear drawings
