How It Works

1. Always recording. Once the camera is on, it records nonstop in the background. Nothing is uploaded; it all stays on this device.

2. Tap Replay after the whistle. It jumps back to just before the snap and plays it in slow motion. The camera keeps recording in the small tile in the corner. Tap that tile, or Live, to go back.

3. Review. Tap the video to pause or play. Drag across it to scrub. Use the arrow buttons to step one frame at a time.

4. Draw. Pick a pen, arrow, circle, or stamp. Touching the video pauses it. Each drawing sticks to the moment you drew it: when the replay plays past that moment, it pauses briefly so the drawing can be seen. Each replay keeps its own drawings. On the live view, picking a tool and touching the screen freezes that moment.

5. Save. Photo saves the frame with your drawings. Save Clip saves the replay as a video. If you drew on it, the drawings are recorded into the video and it pauses for a beat at each one, like a TV telestrator. Everything lands in the tray (grid button). Tap Save there to put it in Photos or Files.

Tips. Hold the iPad sideways. For a full-screen app, use Share → Add to Home Screen. Keep the screen on; the page asks the device not to sleep while the camera is running.
