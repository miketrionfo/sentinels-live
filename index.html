How It Works

1. Always recording. Once the camera is on, it records nonstop in the background. Nothing is uploaded; it all stays on this device.

Record every play. Tap Start Play (big button, bottom right) when the teams line up, and End Play at the whistle. Each play is saved as its own numbered clip on this device. In the tray (grid button), tap ★ on the ones you want to keep, then save them to Photos.

Down & distance. On the live view, tap the down (1st–4th, or Goal for goal-to-go) with your left thumb and slide the yards with your right thumb (+ / − for fine steps). Tapping 1st resets to 10 yards. When you end a play, the down moves on by one automatically (4th goes back to 1st & 10). A gold Undo button shows next to Start Play for 3 seconds if it shouldn't have (timeout, penalty). On a phone, or to edit a play you're watching, tap the gold chip in the top strip. The next play you start gets that label. While watching a play, the chip edits that play. The label goes in the clip's file name, and Save Clip writes it onto the video itself in a gold box.

Last (under Start Play, next to Replay) opens the most recent play straight away. Tapping it during a play ends that play and shows it.

2. Or tap Replay after the whistle. It jumps back to just before the snap and plays it in slow motion. The camera keeps recording in the small tile in the corner. Tap that tile, or Live, to go back.

3. Review. Tap the video to pause or play. Drag across it to scrub. Use the arrow buttons to step one frame at a time. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in on a player, and drag with two fingers to move around. Tap the zoom label at the top to reset.

Zoom slider. On an iPad it sits at the bottom of the left thumb column; on a phone it runs along the bottom. The button showing the level (e.g. 2.0×) resets to 1×. Change where it goes in Settings → Zoom slider.

Zoom while filming. Pinch on the live view. On devices that allow it, this zooms the camera itself, so the recording is zoomed too and the label says "Camera". Otherwise only the screen picture zooms and the recording stays wide.

4. Draw. Pick a pen, arrow, circle, or stamp. Touching the video pauses it. Each drawing sticks to the moment you drew it: when the replay plays past that moment, it pauses briefly so the drawing can be seen. Each replay keeps its own drawings. On the live view, tap Freeze first to draw on that moment. Pinching the live view always zooms; it never freezes it.

5. Save. Photo saves the frame with your drawings. Save Clip saves the replay as a video. If you drew on it, the drawings are recorded into the video and it pauses for a beat at each one, like a TV telestrator. Everything lands in the tray (grid button). Tap Save there to put it in Photos or Files.

Arrange your own controls. On the live view every control is a block on a see-through grid. Tap Layout to edit: drag blocks to move them, ↻ changes shape (or turns a slider), ⤡ stretches sliders, × removes, + Add opens the catalog of every control. Tap Done to save. The layout is saved separately for phone and iPad, upright and sideways, and each block stays pinned to its nearest corner. Export sends your layouts as a file (also copied) a friend can bring in with Import file or Paste. The see-through slider sets how much video shows through.

Keep your plays safe. Plays live in this browser's storage on this device, not in your Photos, until you save them. Safari can erase a website's stored data if the site isn't opened for about 7 days, and clearing website data in Settings erases it too. Two habits prevent losing anything: (1) open the app from its Home Screen icon (see below), and (2) after every game, open the tray, star your keepers and tap Save keepers to Photos.

Add to Home Screen. In Safari: tap the Share button (square with an up arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. In Chrome on iPhone/iPad: tap the Share button in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new Sentinels Live icon from then on. It runs full-screen and keeps its storage much more reliably.

Photos in and out. Save (tray) opens the share sheet; choose Save Video or Save Image to put it in Photos. Import from Photos (tray) brings in videos and pictures from your library, including clips shot with the Camera app's stabilization or Slo-Mo, so you can slow them down, draw on them and save them back. 9:16 (right side, in replay) saves a vertical clip or photo for social media, cropped to what's on screen; zoom in first to frame your player.

Tips. Hold the iPad sideways. Keep the screen on; the page asks the device not to sleep while the camera is running.
